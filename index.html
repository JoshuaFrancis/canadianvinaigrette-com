The method

Fourteen months in oak. Then nothing else.

The vinegar is the product. Every bottle in the collection begins with Quebec ice wine vinegar aged in small-format oak barrels sourced from a cooperage in Bromont. The fruit comes from within one hundred kilometres of the kitchen. The oil is always cold-pressed and pressed in the same season.

There are no thickeners, no gums, no emulsifiers beyond what is already in the mustard. Shake before pouring. Refrigerate after opening. Use within six weeks. These are not shelf-stable condiments. They are perishable by design.

  • ◆ Ice wine vinegar base, minimum 14 months in Quebec oak
  • ◆ All fruit and aromatics sourced within 100 km
  • ◆ Cold-pressed oils only, same-season pressing
  • ◆ No stabilisers, no emulsifiers, no shortcuts

FAQ

Reasonable questions, plain answers.

Where is everything made? +

The vinaigrettes are made in a small kitchen in Dunham, Quebec, in the Eastern Townships. The ice wine vinegar is aged in Bromont. Fruit, aromatics, and oils are sourced within one hundred kilometres unless noted otherwise.

How long do they keep? +

Eight months unopened and stored cool. Six weeks once opened and refrigerated. These are not shelf-stable. Best-by dates are printed on the cap. The Spruce Tip limited edition ships May to July only and keeps four months unopened.

Do you ship outside Quebec? +

Yes. All Canadian provinces in two to four business days. The continental US in three to five. Other regions are case-by-case. Contact us before placing an international order and we will quote freight and customs before you commit.

Restaurant and wholesale accounts? +

Restaurants and independent grocers can request a wholesale rate sheet. Minimum is one mixed case of twelve. Custom label options are available for restaurant accounts on standing weekly orders.
